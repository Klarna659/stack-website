How to choose

  • You only take a GLP-1 and want food logging: Shotsy, MeAgain or Pep put meals front and centre; Stack has no food log.
  • You take a GLP-1 plus supplements, TRT or peptides: an app built for a whole protocol keeps them in one log — that is what Stack is for.
  • You want the lowest cost: Glapp's core tools are free.

About Stack (our own column)

Stack is one log for everything you take, GLP-1 included. Say your protocol and it organises it, sets reminders, projects when each vial runs out, remembers your last injection site, logs side effects with severity, and makes a PDF report for your doctor. $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year in the US, 7-day free trial for new subscribers. It never recommends a compound or a dose.

Not medical advice. Your prescriber decides your dose and schedule; Stack logs what you take.

Questions

Does Stack tell me what dose to take or when to increase it?

No. Your prescriber decides your dose and schedule. Stack logs what you take.

Does Stack log food?

No. If food logging matters most to you, a GLP-1-only app with a food log may suit you better.

Can I export my data?

Stack: a PDF report for any date range and a full backup file. Shotsy's page mentions PDF and CSV exports.
