How to choose

  • You take more than testosterone (supplements, peptides, a GLP-1): an app built for a whole protocol — Stack or Regimen.
  • You want to pay nothing for one compound: Regimen's free plan covers one compound.
  • You want modelled level curves for testosterone and its metabolites on iPhone: OptiPin's page describes that.
  • You prefer a browser to an app: TRT Monitor runs in a web browser.

About Stack (our own column)

Stack is one log for everything you take. You can say your protocol out loud and it organises it, sets reminders, projects when each vial runs out, remembers your last injection site, and makes a PDF report for your doctor. It is a paid app ($9.99 a month or $59.99 a year in the US, 7-day free trial for new subscribers). It never recommends a compound or a dose.

Not medical advice. None of these apps should tell you what dose to take. Dose decisions belong with your clinician, whichever app you use.

Questions

Do any of these apps tell me what dose to take?

Stack does not, and dose decisions belong with your clinician whichever app you use.

Can I export my data from Stack?

Yes: a PDF report for any date range, and a full backup file.

Is my data private in Stack?

Health data is stored in an encrypted database on your device. Cloud backup is optional, off by default, and encrypted on the device before upload. Check each other app's privacy policy for theirs.
