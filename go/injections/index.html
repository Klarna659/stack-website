Stack for Android

One log for your weekly shot.

Stack is one log for everything you take, and it handles the schedules a daily-pill app can't: a weekly injection, every other day, twice a week, every 8 hours. Add your vitamins and supplements next to it.

  • A reminder at the time you set, on the day you set, even with the phone locked
  • Injection-site rotation: tap where you injected and Stack remembers it for next time
  • Pen and vial supply: Stack counts what is left and warns you before you run out
