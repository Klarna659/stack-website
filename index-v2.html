Before you ask

Is Stack free?

Yes, with no cap on compounds. Tracking, reminders, supply projection and rotation cost nothing, with three Sage requests a week. Plus adds unlimited Sage, the in-system levels, bloodwork ranges, full trends, a printable summary of your log and cloud backup.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything on the phone works without one. An account is email-only and exists so a Plus purchase follows you to a new device.

What can I track with Stack?

Anything on a schedule: supplements, vitamins, prescriptions, peptides, GLP-1s and TRT — alongside habits, weight, lifts and bloodwork.

Is Stack on iPhone?

Android first, iOS after. Leave an email and we will tell you the day it lands.

How do I reach you?

Email us and a person replies — hello@trackyourstack.app. Bugs, questions, or anything you want us to build next.
