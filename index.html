Questions

Before you ask.

Is this medical advice?+

No. Stack is a logging tool. It never recommends a dose, a compound, or tells you what to take.

Where is my data?+

On your phone by default. Cloud backup is optional and encrypted, and only runs if you turn it on.

What happens if I cancel?+

Everything you've logged stays readable and exportable. You just stop being able to log new doses.

Does it work for injections, pills and supplements?+

Yes — peptides, TRT, GLP-1s, oral compounds and supplements all log the same way.

iPhone and Android?+

Both. One app, kept in sync with your own encrypted backup if you turn it on.
