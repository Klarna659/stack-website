What this is, and what it refuses to be.

Stack is a logbook and a reference. It records what you took, when, where, and how much of it is left, and it explains what each compound is using public, cited sources.

It does not tell you what to take. It will not suggest a compound, recommend a dose, or read a lab result and pronounce on it. The curve is a model built from published half-lives, not a measurement of your blood. Nothing in the app or on this site is medical advice, and the one thing we will always tell you to do is talk to a clinician.

We would rather state that here than have you find it out on the fourth screen.
