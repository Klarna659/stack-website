01 — The problem

Six things a day is not a checklist problem.

One pill in the morning is a habit. Six things across four schedules — a capsule at eight, a shot at nine, a peptide twice a day, a pen on Thursdays — is a bookkeeping problem, and the notes app was never built for it.

So the questions pile up. Did I already take the magnesium. Which side did I use last time. How many are actually left in that vial. Is Tuesday's shot still doing anything by Sunday. Every one of those is answerable from a record you are already half-keeping.

02 — What the record buys you

A tick says you took it. It cannot say what is still in you.

Every dose you log draws its vial down and adds to a curve. From those two facts the app answers the questions you were carrying: when you run out, and what has not cleared yet.

03 — What it will not do

The refusals are the product.

Anything that records what people take will eventually be asked to advise them. We decided before we shipped where that line sits, and we would rather you read it here than find it on the fourth screen.

  1. It will not recommend a compound. Not by suggestion, not by ranking, not by implication in a list of what other people take.
  2. It will not recommend a dose, or tell you to raise one, lower one, or hold.
  3. It will not read a lab result and pronounce on it. It will put your numbers next to published reference ranges and leave the reading to you and a clinician.
  4. It does not measure anything. The level curve is a model built from published half-lives. It is an estimate, labelled as one, every time it appears.
  5. It is not medical advice — not the app, not this site, not one word of the library. Talk to a clinician about anything you take.

04 — Where the data lives

What you take is the most sensitive list on your phone.

It is encrypted on the device. Cloud backup exists and is switched off until you switch it on. Sign-in takes an email and nothing else, so the account never learns a compound name. You can export the whole record on any plan — your own data is never behind the paywall — and if you delete the account it is gone permanently, not archived.

We would rather hold nothing than promise to hold it carefully.

No ads. We do not sell data and we do not train on it. Analytics are anonymous and aggregate and never include what you track.

07 — Before you ask

Is it actually free?

Yes, and there is no compound limit. Tracking, reminders, supply projection, rotation, streaks and weight trends cost nothing. Plus adds the insight layer.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything on the phone works without one. An account is email-only and exists so a Plus purchase follows you to a new device — it never learns what you take.

What can I track?

Anything on a schedule: supplements, vitamins, prescriptions, peptides, GLP-1s and TRT — alongside habits, weight, lifts and bloodwork.

Will it tell me what to take?

No, and it is built not to. See section 03.

When does it launch?

Android first, iOS after. Leave an email and we will tell you the day it is live.
