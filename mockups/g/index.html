Semaglutide

A GLP-1 receptor agonist taken weekly. Slows gastric emptying and blunts appetite; dose is titrated up over weeks to limit nausea. Half-life ≈ 7 days, so levels build toward steady state over the first month.

Sources
  • Wilding JPH et al. N Engl J Med 2021;384:989–1002.
  • FDA prescribing information, 2024 revision.
  • Knudsen & Lau. Front Endocrinol 2019;10:155.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they start.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your health data is encrypted and lives on your device by default. Cloud backup is opt-in. We never sell it, and never train on it. You can export everything as CSV, and deleting your account deletes all of it, permanently.

Do I need a prescription or a doctor's sign-off?

No. Stack is a personal tracking and education tool, not medical advice. What you track and how you use it is between you and your clinician.

What does it cost?

Free to track, with no cap on compounds. Plus is $9.99/month, $49.99/year, or $124.99 once for Lifetime, and adds the insight layer — curves, bloodwork, unlimited Sage, cloud backup and a doctor-ready report.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

It stays yours. Your logbook and export are never behind a paywall — if Plus lapses you keep your history and can export it anytime.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Stack works offline; backup only syncs if you opt in and you're online.

Which compounds are supported?

112 and counting — from vitamins and supplements to peptides, GLP-1s and TRT — each with a free, cited page in the Library.

Is it accurate?

In-system curves are modeled estimates from published half-lives, not measurements — a way to see trends, never a diagnosis.

What is Sage?

Sage is the optional in-app assistant. It answers your questions in neutral terms and never prescribes or recommends. Free includes 3 questions a day; Plus is unlimited.

When is iOS coming?

Android first, iOS after. Drop your email on the app page and we'll tell you the day iOS is live.
