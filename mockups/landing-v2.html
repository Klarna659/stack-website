Before you ask

Is it free?

Yes, with no cap on compounds. Tracking, reminders, supply projection and rotation cost nothing. Plus adds the in-system levels, bloodwork ranges, full trends, a doctor-ready report and cloud backup.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything on the phone works without one. An account is email-only and exists so a Plus purchase follows you to a new device.

What can I track?

Anything on a schedule: supplements, vitamins, prescriptions, peptides, GLP-1s and TRT — alongside habits, weight, lifts and bloodwork.

Is it on iPhone?

Android first, iOS after. Leave an email and we will tell you the day it lands.
