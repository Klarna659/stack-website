What you actually get

A logbook that does the arithmetic you were doing in your head.

Not another habit tracker with a streak. The point is the four questions you ask yourself anyway — did I take it, when, how much is left, and is it still in me.

01

Two taps, stamped with the time

Open, tap the row. Not a form. The time it went in is the time you tapped, which is the number that matters later.

02

The day you run out, before you do

It counts what you have against what you take and gives you a date, so a refill is something you plan rather than notice.

03

A body map that picks the next site

For anything injected, it remembers where the last one went and rotates for you.

04

An export your doctor will actually read

Every dose, every date, one PDF. Or the raw data, because it is yours.

Where your data lives

On your phone. That is the whole answer.

Cloud backup exists, encrypted, and it is off until you turn it on. There are no ads, and nothing about what you take is sold or shared.

Encrypted on device

Your log is stored encrypted on the phone itself, not on a server that has to be trusted.

Backup is opt-in

Off by default. Turn it on and it is still encrypted before it leaves the device.

Yours to take

Export everything you have logged, any time, in a format you can actually open.
