01

Four questions, answered without you doing the maths.

Everything below is computed from the doses you have already logged. Nothing here asks you to fill in a form.

→Did I take it?

Two taps, stamped with the time you tapped — which is the number every other answer is built on.

→Is it still in me?

A real half-life curve per compound, drawn from your own log rather than from a label.

→When do I run out?

What you have, against what you take. It returns a date, so a refill is planned instead of noticed.

→Where does the next one go?

For anything injected, a body map that remembers the last site and rotates to the next.

04

Your data does not go anywhere you did not send it.

There is no account to make before the app is useful, and nothing about what you take is sold, shared, or used to sell you anything.

01Stored encrypted, on the device

Your log lives on the phone itself, not on a server you have to trust with it.

02Backup is opt-in

Off by default. Turn it on and it is encrypted before it leaves the phone.

03Export whenever

Everything you have logged, in a format you can open without us.

04No ads, ever

The free tier is free because it is a logbook, not because you are the product.
