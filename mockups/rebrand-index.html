Stack · website rebrand

Three ways to make the site look like the app.

All three are full working pages — open them and scroll. Every photograph was generated locally on your own card, at 4K, from the same pipeline that makes the app's backgrounds, so there is no stock licence and no stock tell.

What makes the current site read as generated

  • ×The gold serif italic on the second half of every headline. Three times on one page. It is the single most recognisable AI-landing-page signature there is.
  • ×Gold is dead anyway. You cut it from the app on the 1st. The site is still wearing it.
  • ×Flat black, no photography. The app is photographic; the site is a dark rectangle with cards on it. They do not look like the same company.
  • ×Everything centred below the fold, every block a rounded bordered card, and nothing moves.

Open the live page →

Nothing here touches the live site. These are standalone files in mockups/; index.html and everything it loads is untouched. The photographs live in mockups/assets/ and are regenerated by python tools/gen_header.py — --rank scores each one for whether it actually leaves room for a headline, and reports the contrast every band that carries text would get.

The accent is not a colour I picked. It is sampled out of the photograph by gen_header.accent_from(), which is the same rule the app uses one level down — the app derives its accent from whichever background the user chooses. All five candidate photographs returned nearly the same hue, which is what makes this cold blue a brand colour rather than a preference.
