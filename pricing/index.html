Questions

Billing questions

Where do I subscribe?

In the app, through Google Play. When the iPhone version is out, through the App Store.

Is there a free version?

No. There is no free plan, only a 7-day free trial with everything on. After it, the app keeps everything you logged readable and exportable; logging, reminders and Juno need the subscription.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel in Google Play, or in the app under Settings > Subscription. You keep access until the period you paid for ends.

What happens to my data if I stop?

Everything you logged stays yours: your history stays readable, and the doctor report and a full backup still export. Logging new doses, reminders and Juno need an active subscription. Deleting your own data is never locked.

Do I need an account?

No. Stack works in guest mode. If you sign in, your subscription follows your account across devices. Your health data stays encrypted on your device; cloud backup is optional.
