Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Stack (the “App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is operated by Cedar Hills Studio LLC. Questions: hello@trackyourstack.app.

The short version
  • Your health data lives on your device, in an encrypted database — and stays there unless you turn on Cloud backup.
  • A Stack account is optional — everything on your device works without one; choose “Continue without an account”. If you do create one, you sign in with your email using a one-time code — no password — and the account holds only your email, never what you take (Section 3b).
  • We never sell, rent, share, or harvest your data — not your medications, not your results, nothing. That is the promise.
  • Beyond an account email, if you choose to create an account, features that send data off your device are opt-in: Cloud backup (an encrypted copy for recovery), building your stack with Juno (when the phone cannot read what you typed, said or scanned by itself, that text is sent to be read; never a photo itself; Juno’s chat after setup never leaves your phone), product-update emails, and sending feedback. Details in Sections 3b and 5.
  • Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, and may also be offered through checkout on our website processed by Stripe. Either way, the store or Stripe is the merchant of record and handles your payment details; we receive only your resulting subscription status, never card numbers (Section 8).
  • We show no ads and use no advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking. We do use anonymous, aggregate analytics and crash reporting to improve the App; these never include your health data (Section 3a).
  • The account only knows your email. Your health data stays on your device unless you turn on Cloud backup, or type it into Juno yourself.
  • Label scanning runs on your device; the App does not upload your photos.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Stack mobile application and the Stack website account and web checkout at trackyourstack.app. It does not cover any third-party service you choose to send your data to (for example, a cloud drive where you save a backup).

2. What the App stores on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Compounds/medications, doses, units, titration ladders, schedules, and reminders;
  • Injection sites and rotation history;
  • Vial/supply amounts and projections;
  • Bloodwork/lab results, weight entries, side effects, and notes;
  • Adherence history, streaks, titles/milestones, and app settings.

This is held in an encrypted on-device database (SQLCipher). The encryption key is stored in your device’s secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). We do not have a copy and cannot access it.

3. What we never collect, sell, or share

Whatever you do in the App, we never collect, receive, store, sell, rent, or share:

  • Your real-world identity, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers (a first name you give in setup stays on your phone; the one exception, for signed-in people, is in Section 8);
  • Your health or medication data — what you take, your doses, your lab results, your weight — in a form tied to you (unless you turn on Cloud backup, Section 3b);
  • Anything used to track you across other apps or services.

We never sell, rent, trade, or harvest any of your information, full stop — including anything you send us through the optional features in Sections 3b and 5. We use your data only to run the feature you asked for (reach you about Stack, restore your backup, answer your feedback). By default the App does not transmit your personal or health data anywhere; the only things that leave automatically are the anonymous analytics and crash reports described next, and your subscription status with the store and RevenueCat (Section 8). The optional features that can send data off your device — email, cloud backup, Juno, and feedback — are covered in Sections 3b and 5, and each is off until you turn it on.

3a. Anonymous analytics and crash reports

To see which features are used and to fix bugs, the App uses two privacy-preserving tools. Neither ever receives your health, medication, dose, weight, or personal data, and neither can tie anything back to you.

  • Aptabase (anonymous usage analytics): records anonymous, aggregate events — for example, that a screen was opened or a dose was logged — with no user identifier and no personal content. We only ever see counts, in aggregate. It also records the standard context any app sends (OS, app version, locale). Analytics and crash reports are on in every build and have no off switch.
  • Sentry (crash reporting): if the App crashes, it sends an anonymous crash report with the technical error and your device model, OS, and app version. It is configured to attach no IP address, identity, or data of yours to the report. Like any internet request, it still reaches Sentry’s servers from your device’s network address.

There are no advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking. Think of these as a usage counter and a smoke detector — never a window into your stack.

3b. Your account, and optional features that send data off your device

Your account (optional). You can use Stack without an account — choose “Continue without an account” and everything on your phone works. If you do create one, you use your email and authenticate with a one-time link or code — no password. We store that email and use Supabase (Section 8) to send the code and keep you signed in across your devices and the web. The account holds only your email. Your health data stays encrypted on your device unless you turn on Cloud backup (described below), which stores an encrypted copy — and its decryption key — under that account so we can restore it for you.

Everything below is opt-in. Each one is off until you turn it on, and none is required to use the App. When they do send data, it goes to the provider that runs that feature: Supabase for the updates list, cloud backup and feedback; Cloudflare and OpenRouter for Juno (see Section 8). We use it only for the feature you enabled, and we never sell or share it.

  • Email (product updates): if you choose to join our updates list, we store the email address you enter, the source of the sign-up, and your app/OS version, so we can email you about Stack. No install identifier is sent with it, and duplicate sign-ups are matched on the email address alone. You can leave the list at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us. We do not use your email for anything but Stack, and we never sell it.
  • Cloud backup (optional recovery): if you turn on Cloud backup, we store your email (used to sign you in with a one-time code) and an encrypted copy of your Stack data on our backend so you can restore everything on a new phone. Your data is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. Important, in plain terms: so that we can restore your data even if you lose your phone and every password, the App also stores the decryption key alongside the backup. This means Cloud backup is a convenience feature, not a zero-knowledge one — while we never look at, sell, or share your data, the encrypted copy on our backend is technically accessible to us, unlike the copy on your device. This is a deliberate trade-off for painless recovery. If you want your health data to stay only on your device, simply leave Cloud backup off (it is off by default). You can turn it off and request deletion of your cloud copy at any time (Section 10).
  • Anonymous install identifier: each install of the App generates a random identifier that is not your name and does not identify you in the real world. It stays on your device: the App does not send it with an updates-list sign-up, a cloud backup, or a Juno message.
  • Juno (the in-app assistant): Juno’s chat runs on your phone. What you type to her after setup (log a dose, pause, add, change a time, ask about your log) is read by the app’s own code and never leaves your device. The one place text is sent is when you build your stack (during setup, or “Add your whole stack”): if the phone cannot read what you typed, said or scanned by itself, that text is sent to our processing endpoint on Cloudflare, which forwards it to OpenRouter and on to the company that runs the model, to be read. That text is what you typed, dictated or scanned, and the text your phone read off a label photo you add. The photo itself is never sent — your phone reads it and only the reading leaves. It is all sent without your account, email, or any app identifier; like any internet request it necessarily carries your IP address to the services that process it, but we attach nothing that identifies you, and we do not store it on our backend. The App asks you to allow this, in a one-time prompt, before the first time any of it is sent; you can allow or turn it off at any time from Settings, and adding things by hand always works. Anything you type there is included, so doses, medications and conditions you mention leave your device, and the model providers behind OpenRouter may log requests under their own policies, and we cannot control how long they keep them or whether they use them to train models. If you tap “Report this reply” on something Juno said, that text (and your note, if you add one) is sent to us as feedback so we can fix it. Juno is optional — the rest of the App works without her.
  • Voice dictation: if you use the microphone to dictate to Juno or to speak your protocol during setup, the audio is transcribed by your operating system’s speech-recognition service (Apple or Google), which may process it on their servers. Only the resulting text is used, and we never receive or store your recordings.
  • Feedback: if you send feedback from within the App (including “Report this reply” in Juno), we receive your message, basic technical context (app version, device/OS) and the reply email address if you give one, so we can act on it. Please don't include health details you'd rather keep private in a feedback message.

3c. The email list on this website

The trackyourstack.app website has an optional form to join our email list: news about Stack’s launch, new free tools, and occasional tips. It is separate from your account and from the App.

  • What we collect: the email address you enter, which form you used (for example the launch notice or a tool page), and the date and time you agreed, with the version of the consent wording you saw. Nothing about your health, what you take, or any condition. The form never asks.
  • Why: only to send you the email you asked for, and to prove that you asked. We do not use it for anything else and we never sell, rent, or share it.
  • Double opt-in: the box is never pre-ticked. After you submit, we send one message with a confirmation link. You are added to the list only when you click it. If you do not, the request expires after a week and the address is deleted.
  • Unsubscribe: every email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click, and your mail app may show its own unsubscribe button. When you unsubscribe we delete your address and keep only a one-way fingerprint of it, so we can honor your choice.
  • Deletion: email hello@trackyourstack.app and we will delete your address.
  • Who handles it: Cloudflare (Workers and KV storage) receives the form and stores the list, and Resend delivers the emails. Each is governed by its own privacy terms. The form may also use Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated sign-ups.

4. Camera and photos (label scanning)

The “Scan a label” feature uses your camera, or a photo you choose, and reads the text entirely on your device using Google’s ML Kit on-device text recognition. The image is processed locally to pre-fill fields. The App does not upload your photos, and it does not save the image after reading it.

5. Backups and sharing — you’re in control

  • Local backup file: You can create an encrypted backup file protected by a password you choose. The App writes the file and hands it to your device’s share sheet so you can save it wherever you like. Once you send it somewhere, that destination’s own terms apply. We never receive this file, and we cannot recover its password — keep it safe.
  • Cloud backup (optional): If you turn it on, an encrypted copy of your data is stored on our backend so you can recover it on a new phone with an emailed code. See Section 3b for exactly what is stored and the convenience-vs-zero-knowledge trade-off. It is off by default. You can turn it off at any time, and the cloud copy is removed when you delete your account in the App — or ask us and we will delete just the backup (Section 10).
  • Stack sharing: Stack-share codes/QR are encrypted and shared directly by you with whoever you choose. These do not pass through our backend.
  • Doctor report: the report PDF is generated entirely on your device from your local data, and leaves it only when you print it or share it through your device’s share sheet. We never receive it.

6. Notifications

Dose reminders are local notifications generated and shown on your device. They are not sent through any server.

7. Permissions the App may request, and why

  • Notifications / exact alarms — to deliver your dose reminders on time.
  • Camera — only when you use “Scan a label.”
  • Photos / media — only when you pick a label image or save/share a backup file.
  • Microphone — only when you use voice capture to dictate a log entry. The audio is transcribed by your operating system’s speech recognition service (Apple or Google), which may process it on their servers; it is used only to produce the text and we never store your recordings.
  • Biometric / device credential — only if you enable the optional app lock; this is handled by your operating system and we never receive your biometrics.

You can grant or revoke these in your device settings; some features won’t work without them.

8. Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition) — runs locally for label scanning; the App does not send your images to Google.
  • Aptabase (anonymous usage analytics) and Sentry (crash reporting) — see Section 3a; neither receives your health or personal data.
  • Supabase (backend hosting + account authentication) — stores your account email and sends your sign-in code, and stores the data from the opt-in features in Section 3b (your email, your encrypted Cloud backup, and feedback you send). Governed by Supabase's own security and privacy terms.
  • RevenueCat (subscription status, so a purchase restores on a new device; if you are signed in it also receives your account email and the first word of the name you gave in setup, so trial and renewal emails can reach and greet you, and nothing about what you take) and Stripe (payment processing, if we offer checkout on our website) — used only if you subscribe. Payment itself is handled by the App Store, Google Play, or Stripe, never by us. We never see or store full card numbers. If you enter a creator code, it is saved with your subscription status so the creator can be credited; the creator sees only an aggregate count, never your personal data. Each is governed by its own privacy terms.
  • Cloudflare (request forwarding) and OpenRouter (AI model gateway) — used only for Juno. The text you type, say or scan while you build your stack (never your chat messages after setup), which can include doses and conditions you mention, goes through OpenRouter to the company that runs the model it routes the request to, with no account or identifier attached. Each is governed by its own privacy terms.
  • Your operating system, and any app you choose to receive a shared backup or stack code.

We integrate no advertising and no cross-app tracking SDKs. Beyond the anonymous analytics and crash reporting in Section 3a, the account and optional features in Section 3b, and the messages you choose to send to Juno, the App does not transmit your data.

9. How your data is protected

  • On-device database encryption (SQLCipher); key stored in your OS secure storage;
  • Optional biometric/PIN lock to open the App;
  • Backup files are encrypted with your password before they leave the App.

No system is perfectly secure; you are responsible for securing your device and your backups.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your on-device data is yours to control. You can:

  • Delete individual entries in the App;
  • Use Settings → Data & backup → Delete everything to erase everything on the device (irreversible);
  • Uninstall the App to remove its data from your device.

For data on our backend: you can delete your account directly in the App (Settings → Data & backup → Account → Delete account). That removes your account and its email from our backend and wipes the log on that phone in the same step. A Cloud backup stored under the same email is removed with the account; a backup linked under a different email is not, and the delete screen tells you which case applies before you confirm. You can leave the updates list via any email’s unsubscribe link (unsubscribing stops the emails; to have the stored sign-up record itself deleted, contact us). You can also contact hello@trackyourstack.app and we will delete what you ask us to, including a backup that stays behind. The same steps are on the data deletion page. Purchase records held by the app store and RevenueCat follow their own policies. If you never used those features, we hold nothing to delete.

11. Children

Stack is for people 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13; if you are under 13, do not use the App. If you are under 18, make sure a parent or guardian is fine with you using it. We show no ads to anyone and we never sell anyone’s data. If you believe a child has provided us an email or other data through an opt-in feature, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your privacy rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA-CPRA and similar)

Because your personal and health data stays on your device by default, you can exercise rights such as access, correction, deletion, and portability directly in the App (view, edit, export via backup, or wipe). For any data you sent us through an opt-in feature (Section 3b) — your email or a Cloud backup — you can access, correct, or delete it by contacting us at the email above. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under U.S. state laws. Questions about your rights: contact us at the email above.

13. International users

The App runs locally on your device wherever you are. If you use an opt-in feature in Section 3b, the data for that feature is stored on our backend provider's servers, which may be located in the United States; by using those features you consent to that processing. Everything else stays on your device.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. When we add or change an optional feature that sends data off your device, we describe it here and update the app stores’ data-safety information before that release. Material changes are reflected by the “Last updated” date.
