How to use the checklist

Tick each item as you pack it. The two blank lines are for anything particular to you. Write your questions before the appointment, when you have time to think of them, and leave room under each one for the answer. Use the notes box during the visit for what you were told, or for the name of anything to look up later.

The most useful thing to bring is a current list of everything you take. Fill in the medication list for your doctor and print it with this checklist. If you want a few ideas for what to put on it, see the guide on keeping a medication list for your doctor.

Print or save

Press "Print or save as PDF". On a computer, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to keep a copy. On a phone, use the share menu to save the PDF. Everything stays in your browser and nothing is stored once you close the page, so save or print first.

Not medical advice. This is a blank form for your own records. It does not tell you what to take. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.
