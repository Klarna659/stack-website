Templates

Printable sheets you can fill in on screen. Print them or save them as a PDF. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Printable templates

  • Medication list for your doctorOne page with everything you take. Fill it in, then print or save as PDF.
  • Supplement scheduleA morning, midday, evening and bedtime grid for what you take.
  • Weekly pill chartA seven-day grid with a box to tick for each time of day.
  • Printable medication logA daily or weekly sheet for what you took, when you took it and a note.
  • Medication schedule with a supply sheetA weekly grid or an every-N-days grid, with a supply and reorder line.
  • Injection site rotation chartA printable grid of six general areas with a record table. No drug names, no doses.
  • Printable injection logDate, time, what, area, supply left and notes on one page.
  • What to bring to a medical appointmentA checklist to tick, space for your questions and room for notes.

Calculators and converters

  • Vial run-out calculatorHow many full doses are left in a vial, the date it runs out and when to reorder.
  • Days of supply calculatorHow long what you have on hand will last, and the date to reorder.
  • Syringe units converterTurn a vial strength and an amount into units on a U-100 insulin syringe.
  • Reconstitution calculatorVial size, liquid added and draw amount to syringe units, concentration and draws per vial.
  • Half-life visualiserDraw how much of a dose is left over time, as a percentage of one dose.
  • Vitamin D IU to mcg converterConvert between IU and micrograms on a vitamin D label. 1 mcg = 40 IU.
  • Testosterone ng/dL to nmol/L converterSwitch a lab value between US and SI units. Units only, no ranges.
  • Medication schedule times calculatorClock times for every N hours, every N days or N times a day. Arithmetic only.
  • Glucose mg/dL to mmol/L converterSwitch a glucose reading between units. Units only, no ranges.
  • Cholesterol mg/dL to mmol/L converterCholesterol and triglycerides with the right factor for each. Units only.
