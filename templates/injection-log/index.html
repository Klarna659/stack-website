What goes in each column

  • Date and time: when it happened.
  • What: the name as printed on the label.
  • Area: where on the body, in plain words such as "abdomen, left".
  • Supply left: what is left, counted in whatever you track: doses, pens or millilitres.
  • Notes: anything worth remembering, or a question for your pharmacist or clinician.

There is no column for an amount, because this log is about what happened, not what to do. If you want to keep amounts, write them in the notes exactly as your prescriber gave them.

To work out when a supply runs out, try the vial run-out calculator. To keep track of which areas you have used, see the injection site rotation chart.

Print or save

Press "Print or save as PDF". On a computer, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to keep a copy. On a phone, use the share menu to save the PDF. Everything stays in your browser and nothing is stored once you close the page, so save or print first.

Not medical advice. This is a blank form for your own records. It does not tell you what to take. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.
