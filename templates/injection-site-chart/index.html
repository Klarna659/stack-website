How to use the chart

The chart has two parts. The rotation grid lists six general areas: the left and right abdomen, thigh and upper arm. Each row is one area. Each time you use an area, write the date in the next empty box along its row. After a few weeks the grid shows at a glance which areas you used recently and which you have not.

The record underneath is a plain table: the date, the area and any note, such as how it went or a question to ask later. It is a record of what happened, written in your own words.

Which areas suit your medicine is a question for your pharmacist or clinician. This chart only keeps track of where and when. It does not name any medicine or amount. For more on what to note, see how to keep an injection site rotation record.

Print or save

Press "Print or save as PDF". On a computer, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to keep a copy. On a phone, use the share menu to save the PDF. Everything stays in your browser and nothing is stored once you close the page, so save or print first.

Not medical advice. This is a blank form for your own records. It does not tell you what to take. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.
