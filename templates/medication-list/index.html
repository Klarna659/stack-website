What goes on the list

One line for each thing you take: prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, supplements, and anything you use only now and then. Write the name the way it appears on the label. In the "what it is for" column, a few of your own words are enough.

Put the dose and how often exactly as written on the label or by your prescriber. Add the month or year you started, and who prescribed it if someone did. Use the lines at the top for allergies and for someone to call in an emergency.

Print or save

Press "Print or save as PDF". On a computer, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to keep a copy. On a phone, use the share menu to save the PDF. Everything stays in your browser, and nothing is stored once you close the page, so save or print first.

If you take more than twelve things, use "Add a row". Need to work out when a bottle will run out? Try the days of supply calculator.

Not medical advice. This is a blank form for your own records. It does not tell you what to take. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.
