How to use it

Write each supplement in the first column. In the time columns, write how much you take then, for example "1 capsule" or "2 tablets", or leave the box empty if you do not take it at that time. The last column is for anything you want to remember, such as the brand or a note from the label.

Copy the amount from the label or from your prescriber. This page does not suggest what to take or when. If a label lists the amount in IU or micrograms, the vitamin D converter switches between the two.

Print or save

Press "Print or save as PDF". The sheet prints in landscape, black on white. Your entries stay in your browser and are gone when you close the page, so print or save first. Use "Add a row" for a longer list.

Not medical advice. This is a blank form for your own records. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about what you take.
