How to use it

Each box is one day and one time of day. Write what you take in the box, copied from your label or from your prescriber. Leave a box empty for times you take nothing. The small square under each box is for ticking off once it is taken: tick it on screen, or print the page and tick it with a pen.

Print a fresh chart each week and put the starting date at the top.

Print or save

Press "Print or save as PDF". The chart prints in landscape, black on white. Your entries stay in your browser and are gone when you close the page, so print or save first.

To see how long a bottle of tablets will last at this pace, use the days of supply calculator.

Not medical advice. This is a blank chart for your own records. It does not tell you what to take. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.
