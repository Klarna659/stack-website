Free tools

Calculators for what you track.

Plain arithmetic, done right every time. No signup, nothing sent anywhere, and none of them recommend a compound, a dose, or a schedule.

Reconstitution calculator

Vial size + bacteriostatic water + target dose → the exact units to draw on a U-100 insulin syringe.

Vial run-out calculator

What's in the vial, your dose and how often you take it → full doses left, your last full-dose date, and when to reorder.

Half-life visualiser

A half-life, a dose and a schedule → a curve of how much of one dose is still there over time, as a percentage of one dose.

Syringe units converter

Your mixed vial strength and desired dose → the exact units and mL to draw on a U-100 insulin syringe.
Not medical advice. Every tool here does arithmetic on the numbers you enter — none of them recommend a compound, a dose, or a schedule. Check any change with your prescriber.
