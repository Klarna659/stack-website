How the numbers are worked out

Freeze-dried vials, peptides among them, are mixed with a liquid before use. This calculator does the arithmetic that follows the mixing.

Concentration is the amount in the vial divided by the liquid added. A 5 mg vial with 2 mL added is 2.5 mg/mL, which is 2,500 mcg/mL.

Volume per draw is the amount to draw divided by the concentration. 250 mcg at 2,500 mcg/mL is 0.1 mL.

Syringe units are the volume times 100 on any U-100 syringe, so 0.1 mL is 10 units. Everything else is unit conversion.

Already know the strength of your mix? The syringe units converter skips the first step.

Arithmetic only, not a recommendation. This calculator converts volumes from the numbers you enter. It does not recommend a compound, an amount or a schedule. Your amount comes from your prescriber or the product label.
