How the numbers are worked out

Doses left is what is in the vial divided by (the amount you take plus anything left behind in the syringe on each draw), rounded down to the last whole dose. A 10 mg vial at 1 mg a draw with nothing wasted holds 10 full doses. Add 0.05 mL of dead space at a 5 mg/mL mix and each draw really uses 1.25 mg, so the same vial holds 8.

Runs out is the date your schedule would ask for a dose you no longer have. Reorder by is that date minus your lead time, so a new vial can arrive before the old one is empty.

If you only need to know how long a bottle of tablets or capsules will last, the days of supply calculator is simpler.
