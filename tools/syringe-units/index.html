How the numbers are worked out

Volume is the amount you want to draw divided by the concentration. 250 mcg (0.25 mg) at 2.5 mg/mL is 0.1 mL.

Syringe units are the volume times 100, because a U-100 syringe reads 100 units to the mL by definition. 0.1 mL is 10 units on any U-100 syringe, whatever its size. The barrel only limits how much you can draw at once.

Do not know the concentration yet? The reconstitution calculator works it out from the vial size and the liquid you add.
