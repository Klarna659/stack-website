How the math works

Volume per dose = your dose ÷ the concentration. A 250 mcg (0.25 mg) dose at 2.5 mg/mL is 0.1 mL.

Syringe units = volume × 100, because a U-100 syringe reads 100 units to the mL by definition. 0.1 mL = 10 units, on any U-100 syringe regardless of its barrel size — the barrel only limits how much you can draw at once.
