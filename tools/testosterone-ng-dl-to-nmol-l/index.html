How the numbers are worked out

US labs usually report testosterone in ng/dL. Many other countries use nmol/L. The factor comes from the molar mass of testosterone, about 288.4 g/mol: 1 nmol/L = 28.84 ng/dL.

To go from ng/dL to nmol/L, divide by 28.84, which is the same as multiplying by about 0.0347. To go from nmol/L to ng/dL, multiply by 28.84. The same factor is used both ways, so converting there and back returns your starting number.

This page changes units and nothing else. It does not list ranges, because labs set their own and print them on your report. Use the ones on your report. To keep your results together for an appointment, the medication list template is a good start.
